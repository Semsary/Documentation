Our project is a special web platform called Semsary that helps make it easier to manage short-term rentals in cities. 
Our website will be a place where people can find reliable listings of apartments, learn about fair pricing, and discover essential services nearby. 
It will focus on important things like providing secure rental options, ensuring transparency, and offering verified reviews to make renting safer and more convenient.

Our platform will include tools to suggest the best rental prices, location-based insights for better decisions, 
and ways for landlords and tenants to communicate effectively. It will also have features for filtering properties by important criteria.

Our aim is to simplify the rental process for tenants, landlords, and families by creating a trustworthy, 
user-friendly space. With secure payment options and a way to handle reviews and disputes, we hope to make renting a smooth and satisfying experience for everyone involved.
